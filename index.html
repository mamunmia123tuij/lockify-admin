Setup Wizard

Do the 3 steps below. Each step tests the real service and tells you exactly what to fix. Press the health check any time to see what is missing.

Step 1: Photos (Cloudinary)

  1. Sign up free at cloudinary.com and open the console.
  2. Copy your Cloud name (shown at the top of the dashboard).
  3. Settings (gear icon) → Upload → Upload presets → Add upload preset. Name it lockify_unsigned, set Signing mode to Unsigned, Save.
  4. Type them below and press the button. It uploads a tiny test picture to check everything.

Step 2: Files (Telegram bot)

  1. In Telegram open @BotFather, send /newbot, answer the questions and copy the token.
  2. Create a new channel (menu → New channel). Make it Public for now and pick a username like lockify_store_123. You can make it private again after setup.
  3. Open the channel → Administrators → Add administrator → search your bot → keep Post messages on.
  4. Paste the token and the channel name (with @) below, then press the button. Not sure about the channel? Leave it empty and press Find my channel.

Step 3: Storage limits

Saves sensible defaults if none are set yet (200 MB free per user, 10 MB photos, 20 MB files). The app needs this to count storage.

Lockify Privacy Policy

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

1. Who we are

Lockify (“the app”, “we”) is a password vault and file backup app operated by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY], [COUNTRY]. You can contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL].

2. Information we handle

Account information

Your email address, display name and a unique account ID. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account email and name from Google. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication.

Vault entries (passwords and notes)

Titles, usernames, passwords, website addresses, notes and custom fields that you save in the vault are encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM, with a key derived from your master password) before they are sent to our database. We store only the encrypted data plus a random salt and an encrypted check value used to verify your master password. Your master password is never sent to us and we cannot read your vault. Because of this, we cannot recover your vault if you forget your master password.

Photos you back up

Photos are uploaded to Cloudinary. They are not encrypted on the server. Each photo is checked by an automated safety service (Amazon Rekognition, accessed through Cloudinary) to detect explicit content, and photos that fail the check are rejected. Photo links are long and hard to guess, but anyone who has a link can open the photo.

Files and documents you back up

Files (for example PDFs) are encrypted on your device with your vault key and then stored as encrypted files in a private Telegram channel through the Telegram Bot API. Telegram and we cannot read their contents. Because they are encrypted, we cannot scan them.

Backup details and usage

File names, sizes, types and upload times; how much storage you use; storage you earned or were granted; and, if a photo is rejected, a record of the rejection (used to prevent abuse). We also keep account status information such as whether an account has been suspended.

“Earn storage” feature

When you choose to earn extra storage, the app opens a web page chosen by us inside the app. The operator of that page may receive standard technical information such as your IP address and may use cookies according to its own policy.

Technical information

Our service providers receive technical information needed to deliver the service, such as IP address and device or connection information, when the app connects to them.

What we do not collect

  • Your master password.
  • The readable contents of your vault entries or encrypted files.
  • Advertising identifiers. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.
